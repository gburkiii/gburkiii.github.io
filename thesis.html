always to be found

2025 BFA Thesis Exhibition


Made up of 14 undergraduate seniors at USC Roski School of Art and Design, works in always to be found deconstruct the present to excavate the shells of the past. Along the journey, the sense of self becomes abstracted, unrecognizable, and then found again in the landscape of time.

Participating artists:
Xyla Abella, Leyla Akgedik, Georgia Helena Burki, JD Davillier, Talia Grossman, Mishal Jamal, Kevin Koo, Isa Perez, Sarah Roh, Marina Stamato, Hera Song, Maya Varner, Terrie Yu, Yukin Zhang.

Opening reception: Thurs. April 17, 5 - 7pm
Exhibition on view: Apr. 18 - May 16, 2025
Gallery hours: Mon - Fri, 9am - 4:30pm, by appointment
Gayle and Ed Roski Gallery, Roski Studios Building (IFT)
University of Southern California, Los Angeles, USA
