I love using my hands and my brain to make things, it is a way by which I integrate everything I learn in this life. Fine Art to me is a moving picture, a way to show the world through many different perspectives. It plays on views, emotions, concepts and content. Art-making is a shamanic tool to connect with the spirit of creation and make something of your own. It is a language that I intend to master in this life. Welcome to my site!
